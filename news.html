News

September 2026

MELANOBS presented at the 11th Pacific Islands Conference on Nature Conservation

Thomas Ibanez presented the MELANOBS project during a Species Working Group session of the 11th Pacific Islands Conference on Nature Conservation and Protected Areas, held at the Tjibaou Cultural Centre in Nouméa, New Caledonia (7–11 September 2026).

September 2026

Vanuatu National Herbarium team trained in Nouméa

The team of the Vanuatu National Herbarium (PVNH) spent a week in Nouméa for herbarium management training. They also took part in the resurvey of the Great Ferns Provincial Park plot, and are now ready to resurvey the Nusumetu plot back in Vanuatu.

July 2026

New permanent plot at Vago, Fiji

MELANOBS partners from the Fiji Ministry of Forestry, the University of the South Pacific and the AMAP lab set up a new 1-ha permanent forest plot at Vago, on the island of Viti Levu, Fiji. See the Vago plot page.

March 2026

First resurvey of the Blue River Provincial Park plot

The MELANOBS team completed the first resurvey of the Blue River Provincial Park plot, one year after it was set up. Trees grew slowly overall, except in and around the large gap.

December 2025

First resurvey of the Siloam plot

The MELANOBS team completed the first resurvey of the Siloam plot, one year after it was set up. Trees grew very slowly.

September 2025

New permanent plot at Great Ferns, New Caledonia

The MELANOBS team set up a new 1-ha permanent forest plot in the Great Ferns Provincial Park, on the island of Grande Terre, New Caledonia. Kagus (Rhynochetos jubatus) joined the party! See the Great Ferns Provincial Park plot page.

May 2025

New permanent plot at Poitete, Solomon Islands

MELANOBS partners from Solomon Islands National University, the Solomon Islands Ministry of Forestry and Research and the AMAP lab set up a new 1-ha permanent forest plot at Poitete, on the island of Kolombangara, Solomon Islands. See the Poitete plot page.

March 2025

New permanent plot at Blue River, New Caledonia

The MELANOBS team set up a new 1-ha permanent forest plot in the Blue River Provincial Park, on the island of Grande Terre, New Caledonia. See the Blue River Provincial Park plot page.

December 2024

New permanent plot at Siloam, New Caledonia

MELANOBS partners from the Environment Department of the Loyalty Islands Province and the AMAP lab, with the help of locals, set up the first 1-ha MELANOBS permanent forest plot in New Caledonia at Siloam, on the island of Lifou. See the Siloam plot page.

November 2024

First permanent plot at Nusumetu, Vanuatu

MELANOBS partners from the Vanuatu Department of Forestry and the AMAP lab, with the great help of the Nusumetu community, set up the project’s first 1-ha permanent forest plot at Nusumetu, on the island of Tanna, Vanuatu. See the Nusumetu plot page.

April 2024

Kick-off workshop in New Caledonia

Partners from across Melanesia and beyond spent a week together in Nouméa, New Caledonia (15–19 April 2024), to co-design and launch the MELANOBS network.
